# Independent material-plasma evolution checks

The unmagnetized gamma=5/3 sphere with initially uniform rho=1 and
p=1-s² expands exactly with scale lambda=sqrt(1+2t²). Its material velocity,
surface motion, mass and axial momentum provide an independent continuum test.
The analytic spherical FRC pressure/field pair separately checks convergence
of the initial force residual; initialization in equilibrium is not formation.

Verifies fixed ideal nodal flux labels, the regular-axis affine limit, measured
second-order time refinement of actual gas-expansion energy error and atomic
rejection of a material boundary crossing the fixed magnetic matching sphere.
An independent nonuniform deformation compares flux at corresponding interior
material points and verifies refinement of the nonzero interpolation defect.
Nodal flux invariance is not substituted for that stronger continuum condition.
Source time caps are explicit and negative initial pressure is rejected without
repair. No shock, transport, long-time stability or confinement claim follows.
