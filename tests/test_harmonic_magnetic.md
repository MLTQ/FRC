# Affine magnetic field verification

Uniform physical bias must retain its exact field/energy while producing
identically zero current. Independent analytic polynomial B and curl(B)/mu0
verify current at perturbation amplitudes 1 through 1e-12 beside that bias.
Increment energy is normalized to its own squared amplitude, so the large
background cannot hide an error. Full local magnetic energy includes the
spatially nonzero cross term and agrees with independent physical quadrature;
only its complete-domain integral cancels under the stated boundary conditions.

No input perturbation is computed from numerical current. This tests physical
storage/linear Ampere precision, not nonlinear moving thermal/energy coupling.
