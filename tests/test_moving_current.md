energy norm against the separately assembled original mixed direct solve with
both poloidal/toroidal forcing and varying density. Zero forcing stays zero.
Polynomial regular velocities independently test cylindrical vorticity through
the axis, including rotation and axial shear.

The full induction rate must converge toward this independent Galilean result
on three meshes. This exercises advection of B*, not merely B, and confirms
that retaining current inertia does not spuriously reduce bulk translation.

test does not claim a self-consistent unforced moving-fluid integration.

incompatible radial particle flow is rejected rather than silently clipped.
