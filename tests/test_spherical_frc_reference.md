# Independent analytic free-boundary checks

Finite differences of continuous field/pressure formulas verify curl B/mu0,
J cross B = grad p, and the flux convention at interior and vacuum points.
One-sided approach to the spherical interface checks magnetic continuity,
zero normal field and total traction, without exterior gas or a current sheet.
Axis reversal and the analytic off-axis magnetic null are explicit targets.

Independent tensor Gauss quadrature integrates pressure, interior field energy
and the induced field through all exterior space using x=a/s. Results must
match closed-form continuum integrals. These tests validate a prescribed
equilibrium reference; they do not validate its time evolution, formation,
physical thermodynamic closure, stability or particle confinement.

The actual topology detector must find one O-ring and two physical axis nulls,
then refine the independent spherical separatrix volume approximately at second
order. The largest sampled closed contour remains a smaller lower bound and
must not be confused with the separatrix volume. No noisy/degenerate-feature
robustness claim follows from this clean continuum fixture.
