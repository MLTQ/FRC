# Independent original local transport identity

Manufactures uniform n, species temperatures and axial bias with
v_r=a*r*(1-r^2/R^2), zero J and original ideal E_theta=v_r*B0. Supplies analytic
n_t=-n*div(v), v_t=-v dot grad(v), C_t=s*n_t and the exact Faraday poloidal-flux
rate. The original particle, entropy and total-energy transport identities hold
cell by cell; wall flow and Poynting flux vanish. Polynomial fields are exactly
representable, so every independent cell/face integral must recover the balance.

This is an instantaneous energy-transport manufactured identity, not a complete
finite-mass Ohm solution or a simulated trajectory. Weak Ampere supplies J_t;
its kinetic-energy contribution vanishes at J=0. The test validates physical
flux signs, cylindrical measures, bias work, conservative thermal derivatives
and one-sided face accounting without using the numerical time integrator.
