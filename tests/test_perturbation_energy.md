# Independent thermal energy curvature

Uses 70-digit Decimal evaluation of U(n,C)=n^gamma*exp(C/n), with symmetric
conservative perturbations at step1e-10, to verify the physical second variation.
Different gamma values and correlated density/entropy changes exercise cross
work and the nearly isothermal limit. A separate analytic derivative at fixed
C=2 checks U''(1)/2=11*exp(2)/8 for gamma=3/2. Positivity and invalid-gamma
rejection protect the normalization contract. No simulation is fitted or changed.
