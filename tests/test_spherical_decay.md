# Analytic spherical-decay reference checks

Finite differences independent of the reference derivatives verify flux-to-B,
Ampere current, Faraday electric field and interior Ohm law. Vacuum has zero
current but nonzero Faraday electric field. Regular-axis and continuous dipole
matching are checked explicitly. Independent Gauss quadrature integrates
physical B² and local heat over the complete interior and infinite exterior,
mapped by s=1/x, and checks closed-form totals for two radial eigenmodes.
