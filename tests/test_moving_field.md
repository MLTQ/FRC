# Independent moving-field verification

Checks second-order polygonal material-volume error and exact curved whole-field
sphere volume, symmetric positive magnetic stiffness and independently integrated
physical versus weighted full-space energy. An analytic uniformly magnetized
sphere plus exterior dipole supplies energy and homologous virtual-force targets;
the force target differentiates that continuum formula, not the assembled matrix.

A separate symmetric potential perturbation exercises arbitrary coupled geometry,
vacuum completion, gas pressure, material coefficient and regular-axis chain
terms. Bias source power is checked by an independent time derivative of magnetic
energy. A line integral of r B dot n over a deformed cell verifies the actual
field/geometry derivatives. Invalid matching-sphere crossing and corner inversion
must raise. These checks do not prove general free-surface stability or arbitrary
deformation accuracy; evolving references are tested separately.
