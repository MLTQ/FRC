# Background precision regressions

Uniform density/pressures and a straight uniform magnetic field are an exact
stationary continuum solution. Repeated steps at both prior failing time scales,
both field signs and a stronger background must preserve physical B to roundoff.
The test independently differentiates psi; no assertion of exact zero weak
current is made for a floating-point matrix application.

Independent finite-mass Bessel waves at two very small amplitudes must converge
on three meshes. Errors are relative to the physical perturbation, not the large
background, so a solver that silently drops weak waves cannot pass. The original
variable-density energy, entropy and atomic nonlinear rejection tests remain.
A strong-background endpoint energy replay verifies the explicit roundoff
allowance while an intentionally changed cell energy must still fail the audit.
