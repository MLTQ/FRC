massless electron momentum, **both** thermal equations and full Maxwell/Faraday
with the pressure electric gradient retained. Wall Ez is nonzero while Btheta
vanishes. Species quadratic energies sum to the total acoustic invariant and
zero electron pressure gives the existing cold electric field exactly.
Fourth-order radial differences use h=1e-4 R: the nearly incompressible slow
branch amplifies cancellation if h is unnecessarily tiny. Analytic fields,
not a production spatial derivative, supply all finite-difference samples.

Actual coupled evolution must refine toward the warm pressure solution while
conserving total energy and retaining positive species. Rejection during the
second pressure substep leaves every accepted field/account unchanged despite
prior completed fluid/Hall trial work. A full RHS requires explicit matching
electron entropy, preventing an implicit ideal-only or mismatched derivative.
