# Independent wall and equation checks

At a nonuniform, perturbed two-pressure state, independently reconstructed root
polynomials must give zero physical normal J and toroidal B at the outer wall.
The variational Ampere current must equal the original weak current on admitted
states. Gradient curl, original raw-Faraday reaction work and independently
evaluated physical grid/current/magnetic energy derivative are checked. No
assertion mistakes the nonzero original pointwise curl defect for zero.

Reevaluates every actual nonlinear Gauss stage and endpoint against the complete
collocation equations, with exact inactive wall flow and measured magnetic
trace roundoff. Deliberate nonlinear exhaustion and invalid wall inputs leave
the original state unchanged. These checks do not establish continuum accuracy,
full transits or local charge/enthalpy interface budgets; frozen refinement
studies remain required.
