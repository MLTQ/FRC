# Independent moving-density thermal checks

Tests use degree-six flux polynomials and exact derivatives, arbitrary face
traces, independently constructed scalar weak gradients, actual annular flux
budgets and both physical adjoints. Manufactured conservative particle momentum
supplies the real continuity rate; both gamma choices retain physical entropy
and thermal work during compression. Constant entropy remains exactly constant.

Independent analytic pressure/density gradients check pointwise continuity,
entropy and internal-energy convergence, including the first physical annulus.
Invalid wall flux and underresolved physical quadrature reject atomically. This
suite verifies a common mass/thermal transport component, not momentum coupling,
shock admissibility, a full moving plasma or exact-vacuum formation.
