# Independent skin-response checks

Finite differences of analytic Bessel phasors independently verify flux/field,
Ampere, Faraday/Ohm and cylindrical diffusion identities. The axis is regular;
the prescribed vacuum B is continuous while volume current drops to zero.
Independent Gauss volume quadrature of time-averaged eta J² agrees with inward
surface Poynting power, checking phase, sign and SI normalization.

The radial physical-current discretization is checked against the continuum
derivative in the interior and at the half-cell boundary. The latter is only
first-order pointwise; no false second-order current claim is made. Evolved
fields from physical-only, production UCT and first-order wall-strip methods
must refine toward the independent time-dependent solution. The full one-period
grid/time study is recorded separately rather than hidden in unit tolerances.
These are kinematic conductor checks, not free-boundary or gas-dynamic tests.
