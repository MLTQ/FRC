# Independent analytic induction and atomic failure controls

Manufactures Etheta=x(2-3x²+x⁴), x=r/R, Ez=constant. Cylindrical calculus gives
Bzt=(-4+12x²-6x⁴)/R and Jtheta,t=24x(x²-1)/(mu0 R²); all other magnetic/current
rates vanish. Etheta and Jtheta vanish at the outer wall, psi vanishes at axis
and wall, and the constant Ez checks exact curl-kernel restoration. These
polynomials are represented exactly; weak forcing integrates the independently
derived physical E-I J for actual nonuniform density and physical masses.

Tests compare reconstructed B/J/E with analytic values at physical quadrature,
strict original equation bounds and the original rate-work identity
-Jt.f = Bt.H.Bt + Jt.I.Jt. An initial test draft incorrectly contracted f with
E; that dimensionally inconsistent test was corrected, not a solver bound. They do
not use the solver's discrete operator to define the expected solution. A
one-iteration nonuniform random forcing exhausts the solver without changing
forcing or density. The separate frozen large captured-state probe exercises
the rounding defect and records full residual traces and bitwise replay.
