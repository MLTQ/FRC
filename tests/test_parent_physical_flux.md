# Original species flux cross-check

A uniform physical state with nonparallel velocity/current and nonzero electric,
magnetic and harmonic-bias fields compares the direct parent species flux with
an independently expanded mass-moment expression. It retains all current
advection, current stress and asymmetric cubic drift terms, plus species
enthalpy and full Poynting transport. Particle/current/electron-entropy flux
checks protect units and the species drift signs. No numerical solver is used.
