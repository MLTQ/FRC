# Mixed Euler verification checks

Independently derived conservative and material/adiabatic-pressure manufactured
sources must agree. Exact normal wall velocity and centrifugal/pressure balance
are tested at several actual times. Complex physical EOS differentiation checks
the source work independently of production H gradients.

Analytic even radial polynomials and periodic trigonometric fields verify the
common-grid sampler, including actual angular momentum. Short time-dependent
manufactured evolution must refine against the exact prescribed solution;
an actual rejected forced step leaves its input unchanged. Longer unforced
mesh/time and central-axis controls remain in the saved study, not inferred
from these small tests.
