# Physical centered face verification

Checks periodic uniform translation in both directions: no positive spatial
growth, RK4 amplification bounded at the declared CFL, and preservation of an
arbitrary signal's L2 norm by an independent matrix exponential over forty
transits. Independent analytic cell-average plane waves verify fourth/sixth
spatial convergence. Regular annular powers 0/1/2 test face reconstruction from
physical volume averages through the axis and one-sided boundary stencils.
No positivity or nonlinear stability theorem is inferred from these tests.
