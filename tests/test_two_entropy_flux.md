# Joint physical flux verification

Independent complex-step derivatives of the physical equation of state verify
the H gradient on arbitrary positive states with three velocity components.
The symmetric pair flux is checked against its energy-potential jump identity,
and equal-state flux is compared with the original Euler energy flux.

Independent translating two-temperature contact pressure rates must refine at
high order. Uniform velocity must have zero acceleration without a net-momentum
energy correction. Both species entropy-density and all momenta/density sums
are conservative; true nodal physical energy rate matches a local conservative
flux divergence. Full nonlinear simple-wave refinement and unchanged state on
an actually rejected step are checked. Continuous quadrature aliasing, full
transit, fixed-grid time controls and multidimensional geometry remain separate
required studies; these tests do not certify the full plasma model.
