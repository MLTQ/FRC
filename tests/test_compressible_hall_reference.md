# Independent checks of compressible Hall oracles

Fourth-order finite differences of physical continuum fields verify Ampere,
Faraday, pressure, and original massless-electron / ion momentum residuals.
Both propagation signs, signed bias and all three finite-sound-speed branches
are exercised. No production Hall matrix is the reference for these checks.

Separately eliminated dispersion, ideal-MHD fast/slow/Alfven limits, Cartesian
parallel sound/Hall branches and controlled incompressible limits check the
continuum eigensystem. True physical-volume quadrature checks perturbation
energy at different phases. Axis, ideal conducting-wall traces and the nonzero
resistive-current wall defect are explicit.

Nonlinear manufactured B/J/E derivatives are independently differenced. Exact
Hall work and local Poynting identities include variable density and the axis.
Invalid carrier densities/geometry/modes fail rather than receiving a floor.
These tests validate verification oracles, not a coupled nonlinear Hall solver.
