# Radial mechanical-work and original-equation checks

Separately forms radial-only conservative convective fluxes and continuity,
actual two-species thermal rates and true physical kinetic-energy derivative
for a fully two-dimensional rotating flow. Their global work must cancel with
the derived radial source, independently of the diagnostic source's own residual.

Original cylindrical Euler rates from analytic fields and rotating equilibrium
are checked under mesh refinement, including all three velocity components and
the full annular domain. These do not assert full nonlinear stability, which
requires the separate fourteen-case trajectory protocol.
