About this run and display

The moving outline is the plasma boundary. Physical proportions are preserved. Field lines use interpolation of the saved magnetic field and are display approximations, not particle trajectories. Current colors are physical values; translucency only helps show the interior. The simulation is axisymmetric, not a three-dimensional stability calculation.
