Physical model, source and rendering

The volume is revolved from the actual axisymmetric current. Colors show A/m²; opacity is illustrative, not emitted light. Low-current filtering changes visibility only. Thin interpolation boundary cells are omitted, and vacuum carries no current. Field lines are interpolated paths, not particle trajectories. All directions share one length scale.
