What this run represents

Saved inputs & model

Electrostatic Vlasov–Poisson, actual electron and ion masses, linear tetrahedral fields. All 90 wires and 60 rounded junctions; spherical grounded wall. Steel absorbs particles. No imposed ion well or symmetry filter.

Macroparticle noise, near-wire fields, mesh and timestep sensitivity remain. This is not a fusion-performance prediction.
