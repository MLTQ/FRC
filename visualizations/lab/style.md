# style.css

## Purpose
Responsive dark scientific workspace: fixed experiment/library sidebar, physical
diagnostic cards, 3D/potential views, timeline and a concise model-scope panel.

## Contracts
Shared cyan electrons, amber ions and neutral field/steel colors agree with the
canvases. Numerical controls carry units. Mobile stacks views and converts the
sidebar to horizontal navigation. `[hidden]` reliably removes inactive content.

Saved numerical/physical inputs use a compact definition list, and resolution
notes preserve line breaks without losing their physical-unit labels.

The observable grid pairs field, density, ion motion and population panels on
wide screens, stacking below 1000px. View selectors and scope notes retain room
for the magnetic diagnostic's explicit limitations and numeric units.
