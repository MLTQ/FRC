# Current trajectory display page

Self-contained scientific presentation of four actual saved contact states.
Two synchronized rotatable panels show current magnitude and full-vector
magnetic field lines. Units, physical size, periodic ends, saved time and the
step-18 convergence failure remain visible. The initial view is step 17.

Buttons select saved times without inventing interpolated time samples.
Current color normalization defaults to a common scale; an explicit toggle
enables per-frame scaling to inspect tiny initial currents. B stays on its
common physical range. Animation, when requested, rotates the camera only.

The viewer is entirely offline after assembly, uses no CDN, and retains the
simulation source identity. Opacity, wire cylinder and field-line seed density
are explained as presentation choices. It is a verification fixture, not
coil-driven FRC formation. The 70 mm diameter is a verification dimension,
not a proposed laboratory chamber specification.
