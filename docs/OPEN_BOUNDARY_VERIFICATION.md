Generated by `python -m examples.verify_open_boundary`.

Maximum relative full-space response-energy residual over open pulse cases: 2.21e-14; maximum relative mass change: 2.83e-16. Exterior modal stationarity residual is at most 6.32e-13. Energy beyond the grid is positive and included in induced self-energy.

The budget remains gas plus full-space induced self-energy balanced against applied-field work on plasma current. It does not include external-field self/mutual energy or electrical coil-circuit input. There is no exterior fluid, conductivity floor or energy repair.

**Open magnetic-boundary accuracy has improved; formation accuracy is still incomplete.** Driven peak flow remains resolution-sensitive. The method needs stronger magnetic-shock/skin-layer and compression verification, a moving free plasma interface or a justified physical alternative, realistic gas/end conditions, transport/ionization/Hall validity and converged formation experiments. An analytic magnetic exterior does not provide those physics.
