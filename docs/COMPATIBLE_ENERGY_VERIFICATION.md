# Compatible magnetic-energy verification

Generated by `python -m examples.verify_compatible_energy`. See the [derivation and method contract](COMPATIBLE_ENERGY.md). The historical cell/Riemann/RK2 method remains separately selectable and remains the solver default; all cases here explicitly select their controls.

Positive face-square magnetic energy and natural vacuum boundary work eliminate the identified semidiscrete mismatch for constant bias. SSP RK3 is an optional time integrator. No density/pressure floor, thermal-energy repair, hidden exterior atmosphere or changed shock initial data is introduced. The finite-step response error and physical boundary limitations remain independently measured.
