## Independent expanding-flow solution

Initially rho=1 kg/m³, p=0.4 Pa, vz=±2 m/s, gamma=1.4 and a uniform axial field with vA=1 m/s. The streams separate. Because flow and constant B are parallel, two exact gas-dynamic isentropic fans apply and B must remain unchanged. Only |z|<0.5 m at t=0.1 s is compared, before periodic-boundary shocks arrive. True-vacuum-producing initial data are explicitly excluded from this fixture.

Exact central pressure is 0.0018938734 Pa. Global L1 errors decrease, but the low-pressure central region remains much less accurate than the bulk profile on these meshes. Limiting is inactive in these rarefaction runs: this residual error belongs to the underlying Rusanov discretization, not a pressure floor. It must remain visible when choosing resolution and interpreting rarefied plasma regions.

Maximum relative mass change across completed cases: 0. Maximum relative total-energy change: 1.62e-16.

The uniform parallel flux is unchanged to floating-point accuracy. Cell density and pressure stay positive without repair.
