# Independent nonuniform-contact mode

Loads the hash-verified frozen N=64, first-Bessel-mode eigenvector from the
independent original continuum operator. Uses deterministic real sign and
maximum scaled amplitude, with no production-space eigenmode fitting.
Fourier amplitudes and exact J0/J1 fields provide density, velocity, both
specific entropies, B, strong Ampere J and poloidal flux. The source even
Nyquist convention is retained; magnetic divergence is measured, not assumed.

Full linear electric field follows original ideal, Hall and physical inertia
terms plus sum a_s/e [grad(dp_s)/n-dn grad(p_s)/n²], a=(-beta,alpha).
Here dp_s=p_s(gamma_s dn/n+ds_s); background n/p gradients and perturbation
Fourier derivatives are differentiated explicitly. There is no discrete curl,
weak projection, numerical mass adjustment or fitted electric field.

The source spectrum is a converged continuum discretization, not a laboratory
stability prediction. Growth is ~8763.749/s for this fully ionized argon test.
Comparisons must retain representation error, physical divergence, mesh and
finite-amplitude controls, full E and both thermal species. No simulation
state is constructed or evolved by this independent reference module.
