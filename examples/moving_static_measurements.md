# Independent static moving-field accuracy studies

The uniform interior field with a vacuum dipole has full-space induced energy
E=pi (1/lambda - 0.6 lambda + 0.09 lambda³) joules for the stated B0=sqrt(mu0),
initial unit radius, fixed ideal flux and bias=0.3 B0. Its analytic derivative
supplies an independent homologous magnetic-force target. Neither target uses
the assembled magnetic operator. Axial translation leaves this continuum energy
unchanged, so nonzero net axial force measures mesh/matching error.

Records 27 static evaluations across material mesh, deformation, translation,
infinite-vacuum modes, matching sphere and quadrature. The full measured source
and environment fingerprint is retained; changes during a run are rejected.
Physical B² energy and weighted a-energy are independently integrated.

This is an electromagnetic virtual-work fixture, not a dynamically valid
free-gas equilibrium with its nonzero normal field and tangential discontinuity.
The separately initialized spherical FRC supplies the free-interface force
balance reference. Modal stationarity alone cannot establish vacuum accuracy.
