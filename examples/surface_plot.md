# Scientific surface-resolution figures

Plots actual saved equatorial velocity, P0 pressure/density, weak nodal toroidal
current and surface velocity for uniform and graded meshes plus angular control.
Reference material radius is the comparison coordinate; final deformed position
is used when forming physical Jtheta. A separate panel retains large pressure/
magnetic cancellations and the independent discrete volume-force diagnosis.
Cell centers are reference Q1 centers, with no smoothing of thermal arrays.

The accuracy figure separates independent continuum magnetic/current/local-heat
and gas-expansion errors from adjacent-mesh driven-flow differences. Grading
does not change D. Both figures require visual inspection before delivery;
plot/force/analysis sources are hashed separately from scientific measurements.
The profile preview and assembled profile figure have been visually inspected:
axes, units, cell values and the force-cancellation panel are readable. The
accuracy figure suppresses minor logarithmic x-axis labels so they cannot
collide with the explicit measured mesh sizes. Both final assembled figures
were visually inspected, including the corrected logarithmic axes.
