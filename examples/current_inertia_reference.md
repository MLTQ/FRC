# Independent non-Beltrami nonlinear inertia reference

Differentiates the original positive varying-density toroidal field analytically.
Returns w, curl w, full -(mu delta/e)(w dot grad)w, its equivalent reduced
delta w cross (mu curl w/e), and physical kinetic scalar gradient. This field
does not have the exact helical wave's nonlinear cross cancellation.

All scalar formulas remain holomorphic for independent complex-step evaluation
of the physical cylindrical curl; Gauss points avoid dividing at r=0. Both full
and reduced electric fields yield the same g-rate, with the correct gradient
identity and finite regular-axis limit. No production current/projection/curl
operator enters this reference. The measured quantity is nonlinear forcing of
B*, not a complete B evolution after the inertial elliptic solve.
