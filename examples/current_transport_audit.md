# Current-transport evidence audit

Requires every protocol case with matching source/raw hashes, then replays all
saved arrays and metrics bitwise on the original numerical platform (elapsed
time excluded). Independent root-polynomial/FFT reconstruction integrates local
force/electric errors and physical power at orders 16 and 20. Writes incremental
nonterminal progress and a terminal audit only after every case completes.

Reports quadrature differences, independently integrated electric/current/
magnetic work versus production, and continuous versus grid stress/total power.
Relative differences of near-zero integrals can be large from cancellation;
absolute differences and scaling by the total absolute physical power are also
recorded. These are measurements, not automatic approval thresholds. Successful
replay alone does not certify convergence or formation. Review the measured
protocol and conservation defects before any solver adoption.
