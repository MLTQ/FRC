# Continuum variable-density component reference

An independent analytic smooth periodic cylinder has psi=a x(1−x/X)^3
(1+epsilon cos kz), g=G(1−x/X)^2(1+epsilon sin kz), and a retained uniform axial
bias. Its current comes from the analytic cylindrical curl. A polynomial radial
envelope satisfies axis/fixed-flux and natural Btheta wall conditions. Analytic
Etheta=−psi/(r tau), Ez=−G X(1−x/X)^3(1+epsilon sin kz)/(3 tau) give
B_t=deltaB/tau and J_t=J/tau without referring to a discrete operator.

Density has radial/axial variation. Its rate is the negative analytic divergence
of prescribed particle flux nv_r=n0 V r/R (1−x/X)^2 cos kz,
nv_z=n0 V(1−x/X) sin kz, with impermeable radial walls. Surface-integrated
fluxes generate the supplied discrete continuity rate in the driver.

The declared manufactured electric forcing is
F_other=E−mu J/(n e² tau)+mu J n_t/(n² e²). It forces the original instantaneous
moving-density induction component. It is **not** a parent-species solution or
a full moving momentum/thermal experiment. Analytic fields are used only as
initial data, declared manufactured source and error targets, not as replacement
current or magnetic fields in the discrete density force.

`thermal` separately gives a nonbarotropic smooth pressure and fixed-n entropy
drift target J dot grad(U/n)/e. This probes the lumped graph pressure bridge's
remaining consistency error, not the manufactured induction equation's source.
Electron and ion gammas can be assessed separately.
