# Independent physical norms

Field sampling uses root-product Lagrange/histopolant bases, independent of the
production scaled Vandermonde solves. Density sampling is separately reconstructed
by differentiating a root-product polynomial through **cumulative analytic cell
integrals**, not by reusing production moment quadrature or maps. The same stated
mirrored stencils define the representation being audited.

Physical quadrature compares B, J, B_t, J_t, E, n, n_t, both species pressure
fields, current energy and separate local magnetic/current/density energy rates
against the continuum reference. Every norm exposes relative L2, absolute RMS,
near-axis relative L2 and sampled maximum. Cell energy integrals are returned for
comparison with production at independently increased quadrature. Tiny/net-zero
global energy rates are not used as the only accuracy denominator.
