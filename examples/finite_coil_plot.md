# Saved finite-coil response figure

Presents two completed trajectories and the fine trajectory's actual6us frame.
Current is the same weak-Ampere current reconstructed from saved coefficients;
B is the saved induced field plus analytic finite-coil source. Independent
root-product polynomial evaluation samples the displayed meridional field.
Contour lines are constant axisymmetric poloidal flux; no synthetic torus or
formed FRC is drawn. Their geometry is not evidence of3D stability/confinement.

Outputs PNG/PDF, sampled profile and source/run/frame references. The figure
labels fixed sealed geometry and the actual drive strength. Only terminal
successful runs are accepted. It does not rerun a plasma trajectory, alter
states or make a physical parameter recommendation. This is one scientific
figure for completed solver work, not a verification/reporting framework.

The field history compares the same plasma-volume mean with and without the
plasma response; it does not compare a mean to a central point value.
