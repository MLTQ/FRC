# Slower-branch finite-amplitude controls

The main coupled study retains slow-branch instantaneous errors that reach a
floor with mesh refinement. These six additional independent parent-rate cases
reduce continuum linear-wave amplitude by factors ten and one hundred, on
sixteen/thirty-two element meshes, for each slower branch. They distinguish
finite-amplitude nonlinear residual from spatial consistency error.

Each full array and non-time metric is recomputed bitwise, then independently
integrated at orders 16 and 20. The source closure extends the complete coupled
protocol; records are immutable. No slow-branch trajectory, error removal or
formation claim follows from an instantaneous amplitude comparison.
