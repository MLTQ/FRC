# Smooth transport comparison protocol

31 rates cover the previous 21 cases, two further tenfold amplitude reductions
and magnetized contact rates with smooth/limited entropy on four axial meshes.
27 trajectories retain all 18 baseline inputs/time controls unchanged, then add
three unmagnetized full-transit contacts, a limited-entropy comparison, reverse
flow, half time step and three short magnetized contacts.

The unmagnetized contacts test nonlinear thermal advection over one transit
without the explicit Hall stiffness. Their timestep uses an explicit small
acoustic/advective cap; accuracy is checked by halving. They do not replace the
original-density magnetized rates or 50 ns magnetized contact controls, nor do
short magnetized trajectories establish a full magnetized transit or stability.
All cases state their field, density, duration, diffusion choice and actual mesh.
Old 21/18 records remain measurements of their original source and method.
