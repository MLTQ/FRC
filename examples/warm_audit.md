# Warm study audit

Checks exact declared case coverage, frozen sources, strict JSON equivalence,
archive hashes, coordinates, volume conventions, positive states and every
accepted history count/time/energy/entropy. Initial conservative quadrature is
independently replayed at order 18. Incident quarter-cell loops independently
recover dual n and volume; explicit corner sums recover primal electron energy.
Direct physical Gauss integration of the stored flux field replays magnetic
energy using the earlier independent `magnetic_product` audit, not the solver's
energy routine. Species energy and logarithmic entropy are independently replayed.

Q1 electron and reconstructed ion physical error integrals are compared at
orders 8 and 12. Their perturbation norms and SI errors are separate from
node/cell errors. The report must hash this audit, norm module and imported
independent magnetic audit as well as its plotting source.

An explicit supplemental configuration list is supported without weakening case
coverage; its caller additionally checks the separately frozen control driver.
Compact scalar and nested limiter history columns are length-checked and
inverted before all per-stage assertions, preserving every accepted value.
