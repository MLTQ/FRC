# Toroidal thermal principal matrices

Extends the pressure-only symbol to exact axisymmetric purely toroidal B with
zero bulk background flow. Hall induction is

    Bt_t|Hall = Bt/(mu0 ne) [(a_r+2/r) Bt_z - a_z(Bt_r+Bt/r)], a=grad log n.

Its linear derivative coefficient H enters the magnetic diagonal of the joint
fixed-density [Bt,pe] symbol. The moving massless model adds continuity,
longitudinal ion velocity, both pressures, and density-gradient perturbations
in Hall and pressure induction. The 5×5 matrix acts on [n,v_parallel,pi,pe,Bt];
transverse flow has zero principal speed and can be omitted at zero flow.

Purely toroidal B/axisymmetric perturbations form an invariant sector. Hall's
usual second-derivative term cancels there; gradients and cylindrical curvature
contribute to the remaining first-derivative linearization. Nonreal eigenvalues
therefore signal unbounded growth in this local frozen sector. This is not a
nonlinear general well-posedness theorem or proof of a particular discrete
axis failure. Matrices include background current advection and all density
derivative terms. Acoustic/magnetic scaling is only a similarity transform.
