# Cylindrical standing-wave reference

Small-signal analytic radial mode: delta n/n0=epsilon J0(kr) cos(ckt),
vr=epsilon c J1(kr) sin(ckt), kR=first positive zero of J1. Density is even
and radial velocity odd at the exact axis; the outer wall has zero velocity.
Both species have their original isentropic EOS. One full acoustic period is
the trajectory duration. This is a **linearized** PDE reference: finite-amplitude
nonlinear evolution is only expected to approach it as epsilon decreases.
Error relative to the perturbation and an amplitude control are required;
small errors normalized by background density alone are insufficient evidence.
