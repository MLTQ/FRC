# Hall record validation and scientific figures

Requires exact protocol coverage, unchanged production/reference sources, complete
step histories, matching per-case/collected records, finite lossless fields,
positive heat and verified archive hashes. Parallel-mode coverage and its sources
are checked separately. Does not rerun or alter failed/incomplete physics cases.

Plots measured cylinder field and phase convergence, actual semidiscrete time
error, independently derived parallel dispersion with evolved sample frequencies,
energy residual and parallel time convergence. A separate actual-cylinder field
image shows regular toroidal magnetic field and bulk rotation before/after the
wave, with identical before/after color limits for each quantity. The accuracy
figure explicitly labels the different fixture radii. Color maps plot unsmoothed sampled toroidal fields. Figures and report
sources are hashed separately from the measurement record. Results and physical
limitations are described in LINEAR_HALL_VERIFICATION.
Mesh/time plots label the actual tested counts and suppress colliding minor
tick labels; no data are removed.
