# Independent continuum energy quadrature

Gauss integration over true spherical radius and polar cosine integrates
analytic B, me J²/(2 ne e²) and local heat. An exact dipole formula adds the
exterior magnetic energy to infinity; prescribed uniform bias is subtracted
before forming induced magnetic energy. There is no numerical-mesh input.

The study compares orders 48 and 96 and checks the closed-form uniform-decay
energy separately. Variable-density and screening references use their actual
physical carrier profiles. Screening is evaluated at its specified final bias;
its initial zero energy is handled separately by the measurement recorder.
