# Parent-species and generalized-field audit

Checks exact reduction for physical Ar+ at n=1e18 and 1e20, a physical pair
plasma and the explicit consistent massless control. Includes points approaching
the regular axis. Parent continuity, momentum, electric difference and Faraday
residuals are compared independently; full energy flux is checked against direct
species kinetic/enthalpy sums. The B* velocity-vorticity form must agree with
the original convective drift equation, including n_t/n and both scalar gradients.

Fourth-order physical differentiation of total species flux checks the local
energy law at three spacings. Omitting the mixed mu n (v dot w) w energy flux
is a separately measured incorrect-model control. Absolute magnitudes of the
drift-stress, density and velocity-curl contributions expose terms that would be
hidden by a residual normalized only to a much larger bulk background. The
reference's explicit force/heat remain included; no unforced-equilibrium claim.
