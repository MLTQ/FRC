Uses audited records only. Shows full pressure-rate errors, evolved manufactured
pressure with both background and physical-change normalization, fast-wave
perturbation errors and the central/diffusive/cubic continuity diagnosis. All
norms and candidate scope stay explicit; no topology or formation image is used
as scientific validation. Matplotlib Agg supports headless reproducibility.

The report hashes generated figures; a human-visible image inspection is needed
before the checkpoint calls them inspected. The plot is not a test threshold.
