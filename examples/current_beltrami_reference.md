# Nonlinear finite-mass Beltrami reference

An exact finite-amplitude wave for the constrained zero-barycentric-flow,
uniform-density current subsystem. Independent cylindrical Bessel functions
give curl b=h K b and B=B0 ez+b. The nonlinear cross term J cross b* vanishes,
leaving omega=delta h B0 kz K/[mu0 ne(1+d*² K²)]. Both signs and massless
control are explicit. Neither electron mass nor the time mass is fitted.

Full E is independently calculated from generalized species momentum,
including mu/e [w_t-delta (w dot grad)w] and cylindrical convective terms.
The mass-proportional reaction needed to hold bulk flow zero is exposed. Both
individual species equations and zero total reaction power can therefore be
checked independently. Species pressures are uniform/constant; drift velocity
is divergence free. Magnetic traces match the declared zero-Btheta wall.

The wave is finite amplitude, not a small-amplitude linearization. Uniform
bulk flow is constrained externally for this component; the reaction is not
a claim that the physical plasma would stay stationary. `initial_fields`
integrates true annular g means at arbitrary time without fitting pressure or
energy. Vector-potential amplitude is in T m, and frequencies are signed.
