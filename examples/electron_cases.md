# Electron-pressure protocol

The declared protocol covers physical current, analytic Biermann/entropy spatial
rates, regular axis and wall, uniform-density/isothermal zero-battery controls,
stronger density contrast and nondefault gamma. Actual evolution includes rigid
electron heat translation, both signs and amplitude stress, spontaneous pressure
battery creation from g=0 and nonlinear feedback from finite g.

Independent fixed-grid time, arbitrary proxy-density-scale and nonlinear
tolerance controls are included. The strict nr64 tolerance control intentionally
retains a finite-precision Picard failure near the regular axis; no failed trial
is repaired or silently rerun with a changed tolerance. Default 1e-10 is explicit.
The protocol is a fixed-density isolated pressure/entropy component study,
not a warm compressible Hall or physical plasma-edge experiment.
