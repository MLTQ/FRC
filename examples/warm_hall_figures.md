# Warm Hall scientific figures

Exports two static publication-style PNGs from audited stored measurements.
Six convergence panels retain every wave branch, continuous species/field
norms, pressure-work/axis error, the nonconvergent resistive axis derivative,
and stationary-ion RMS/maximum spurious heat. The controls figure contrasts
slow/fast actual wave profiles with the independent linear reference, reports
time differences and explicitly labels the total-energy conservation budget.
No field is smoothed for the measured curves; smooth lines are analytic oracles.
Figures must be rendered and visually inspected before checkpoint delivery.
