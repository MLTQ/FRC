# Independent original transport terms

Analytic density, current and particle flux come from the original cylindrical
curl fixture. Velocity includes the independently prescribed rotation used in
the coupling study. Complex differentiation of these original physical fields
gives covariant velocity/drift gradients and divergence of the physical drift
stress. The exact axis divergence uses the analytic derivative limit, not a
small positive radius. These are individual parent-equation terms, not solver
residuals or a manufactured full time trajectory.
