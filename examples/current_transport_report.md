# Measured transport report

Reads the immutable sixteen terminal records and their complete independent
audit. Produces a compact summary plus PNG/PDF convergence panels for electric
terms, continuous drift forces, axis/wall accuracy, continuity defects, face
omission controls and grid versus continuous instantaneous energy rates.

Rendering only: no solve, repair or interpretation as a trajectory. Near-zero
power comparisons retain absolute and physical-rate-scaled errors; local force
norms come from independent continuous reconstruction. The report is outside
the frozen calculation closure and must not modify any study record.

The energy panel includes degree-three continuous error to expose the measured
grid/continuous distinction. Minor x labels are suppressed on logarithmic axes
so the four actual resolutions remain legible.
