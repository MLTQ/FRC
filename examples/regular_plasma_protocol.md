# Immutable coupled verification protocol

Twenty-five cases: sixteen independently differentiated contact/wave rates with
mesh, amplitude, branch and quadrature controls; seven unforced full fast-wave
periods with mesh/time/amplitude controls; two separate full stationary spectra
with nonlinear perturbation controls. Both slower branches retain finer mesh
rate controls. The failed nonlinear coordinate-difference spectrum attempt is
kept in regular_plasma_coordinate_difference_attempt, including its complete
source and first nineteen successful cases. Reused records explicitly name that
origin and are recalculated under the final source during the full bitwise audit.
The long nonlinear contact trajectory is
not included or asserted complete. Its stiffness needs separate time-method work.

Execution saves exact source/runtime, full arrays and metrics, refuses record
overwrite, preserves terminal failure and checks source identity again at exit.
Audit repeats every calculation bitwise, checks final trajectory steps, and
independently reconstructs rates/final fields at quadrature orders 16 and 20.
Progress is resumable evidence, not a terminal audit. Full parent local
enthalpy/Poynting budgets and exact vacuum/formation remain separate gates.
