# Parallel-wave time and dispersion measurements

Runs 16 Cartesian circular modes: massless Hall and finite-electron-mass models,
both helicities and k di=.1/1/10/1000. Each runs 40/80/160 steps over two radians
of its independent continuum frequency. Saves phase, damping, full-state errors,
heat and energy residual, refinement ratios and final complex coefficients.
This deliberately has no radial discretization error and is labeled as the
parallel limit, not an axisymmetric cylinder wave. Real-sinusoid averaged energy
has units J/m³; the positive quadratic current energy is included. Complete
coefficients and relevant source hashes are stored separately from cylinder data.
