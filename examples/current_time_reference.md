# Independent semidiscrete time audit

Dense elimination of stationary vacuum and a generalized symmetric eigensolve
construct a spatial eigenmode independently of the mixed step solver. Its exact
decay rate is D k_h²/(1+de² k_h²), bounded by D/de² at high wave number.
Three time steps at fixed geometry compare actual evolved current amplitude
with this exponential. This verifies temporal order independently of spatial
interpolation error; it does not replace continuum sphere verification.
Full inputs and ratios are retained in the final study, and this helper is
hashed separately from the frozen 35-case measurement protocol.
