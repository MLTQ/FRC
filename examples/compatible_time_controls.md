# First-order boundary time controls

The identical nr=192 shock is rerun with first-order spatial fluxes and the
compatible staggered/variational/RK3 choices at CFL .2 and .1. This separates
time sensitivity of the more diffusive baseline from the second-order/local
fallback trajectory; it does not replace the independent high-order accuracy
requirements with an easier fixture.

Complete records and accepted-state progress use the common measurement helper.
Engine/helper, this supplemental protocol and environment hashes guard reuse.
Supplemental files remain separate from the already frozen 25-case protocol.
The final report consumes both sets with their distinct provenance.
