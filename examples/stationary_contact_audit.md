# Replay and independent spatial controls

Replays all three frozen 360-coordinate probe arrays/metrics bitwise. The
uniform probe's maximum-real eigenvector lies in the numerical nullspace, so
its original relative mixed-direction error is ill-conditioned; three nonzero
random directions are instead compared with the separately derived stationary
parent operator. The original null-mode result is retained, not silently fixed.

Extracted blocks and a paired species-pressure/entropy removal are diagnostic
ablations of the saved Jacobian. They are deliberately incomplete equations,
never candidate physical evolutions. Their eigenvalues localize which feedback
is necessary for rapid growth; they do not identify its mathematical cause.

The separate original-equation Fourier/Bessel operator is controlled by all
three uniform wave branches, axial refinement at the first Bessel mode, several
higher Bessel modes and leading-mode magnetic-divergence/eigenpair checks. Saves
all matrices, eigenvalues, scales and leading vectors with source/raw hashes.
Its radial subspaces differ from the production probe. Spectral growth values
must not be claimed a matched-mode comparison or proof of numerical versus
physical instability until further projection/resolution/evolution controls.
