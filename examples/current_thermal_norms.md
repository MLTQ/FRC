# Independent physical current/thermal norms

Direct formulas sample bilinear flux in x=r²/2,z, Btheta=r g, and the declared
electric/current basis. They do not invoke production sampling, energy, mass,
curl or flux maps. Physical r,z Gauss integration replays magnetic, current and
both thermal energies. Q1 nodal pressures and density remain distinct from
nonlinear functions of sampled density. Independent quadrature orders expose
integration uncertainty, particularly in variable-density kinetic energy.

Exact finite-amplitude wave errors use the perturbation B and full physical J
as denominators, with absolute T and A/m² errors and first-annulus current error.
The reference kinetic energy uses physical reduced mass. Full continuum energy
flux includes E cross B, both species enthalpies and drift kinetic transport;
face integrals have units W and include all boundary/seam faces. These routines
measure errors and cannot repair or change the numerical trajectory.
