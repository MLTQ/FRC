# Audited variable-density report

Requires all 14 raw cases and their independent replay audit, checking hashes
before assembling a compact report and four-panel scientific figure. Rate and
local energy convergence are separate from the pressure bridge's physical
pressure/entropy errors. The pressure-work panel explicitly labels its different
scales: old-stream relative net-work mismatch versus new noncancelled backward
error. It must not be interpreted as physical pressure accuracy.

The report helper and companion have their own frozen archive/hash, outside the
earlier production/measurement source identity. The figure is intended for visual
inspection and the report retains all numeric errors and controls. No production
threshold or original induction tolerance is altered by report assembly.
Only the four measured radial resolutions are labeled on logarithmic x axes;
minor tick labels are suppressed to avoid overlapping scientific notation.

`thermal_regions` independently compares saved nodal entropy rates to the
continuum target and records radial squared-error profiles. Disjoint first-four,
interior and last-four radial-node regions expose their error fractions, local
relative L2, absolute RMS and maximum; this diagnoses axis error without silently
redefining the published whole-domain norm. The first-four region shrinks
physically as the mesh refines, which must be considered when interpreting it.
