# Complete independent-support species expansions

Runs the same neutral, charged and physical-mass electron–ion problems as
examples.atomic_moving at32/64/128 cells per occupied species. The sole intended
change is the transport/front discretization. This is a reaction-disabled
longitudinal fluid component, not a replacement for the formation solver.

Neutral: n=1e19/m³, a=25mm, T=0.1eV, initially at rest. End time a/(4c) precedes
interaction of the two rarefactions. The independent Euler vacuum fan has
n/n0=[2/(gamma+1)(1-(gamma-1)xi/(2c))]^(2/(gamma-1)) and front a+3ct.
Warm charged slab: n=1e12/m³, a=25mm, T=1eV[1-(x/a)^2], end at analytic s=1.5.
The existing independent scalar energy-integral quadrature supplies its time;
no discrete-solver trajectory is used as a reference. Initial cell temperatures
are exact quadratic averages. Density, field and both front errors are measured.

Plasma: n_i=n_e=1e14/m³, a=10mm, Ti/Te=0.2/2eV, full20ns request on every mesh.
Physical argon ion and electron masses and initial E=0 match the previous case.
Each species has its own actual support. Finite positive particle amounts belong
to material cells; no numerical ion tails are generated by cross-species ALE
transport. No collisions, reactions, atmosphere, remap, floors or repairs.
Numerical viscosity is OFF by default, explicitly reported.

All accepted x/v/U states and physical total-energy/momentum histories are saved.
Field energy includes the exactly empty exterior. Density errors integrate over
both numerical and analytic support using16-point Gauss on their combined
cell/front/head intervals. Plasma comparisons require completed equal-time states
and report separate electron/ion density/current/field and front differences.
Failures retain every accepted state and the attempted-step reason. A failed
case stays failed; neither settings nor states are repaired.

Usage: `python -m examples.species_material FRESH_OUTPUT` in the usual headless
plotting environment. No unit tests, new report framework or imposed pass gate.
Converging front positions and local fields must be assessed alongside budgets.

`--mesh cosine` redistributes the same number of material labels toward both
free surfaces: x_j=-a cos(pi j/N). Particle amounts remain exactly n0 times
each initial physical width; no density profile, total inventory, temperature
law, mass or requested duration changes. Uniform mass cells resolve the low-mass
neutral rarefaction tail slowly; this explicit spatial choice evaluates edge
resolution without estimating/forcing an analytic front trajectory.

`--viscosity C1 C2` enables the compatible compressive stress documented in
species_material.md. An independent trapezoidal history reports its numerical
heating separately; it never repairs total energy. This evaluates unresolved
compressive oscillations, not physical heat transport. `--step-scale 0.5`
halves both actual acoustic/plasma time limits for an equal-input time comparison.
All requested durations and physical initial data stay fixed.

`--cells 128 256 512` extends the same complete physical studies after the
original32/64/128 histories. Adjacent-grid comparisons and plots use the explicit
counts, with no shortened duration or altered mass ratio for refined cases.
