# Current and transport scientific figures

One figure separates continuum decay B/current/heat errors, independent
collisionless current errors, actual equatorial current and central penetration.
The thin screening layer includes its coarse-grid artifacts without smoothing;
the report explicitly quantifies false reversal instead of inferring topology
from the energy budget. Zero de is a declared massless comparison.

The transport figure plots conditional argon scales and a prescribed-density
edge against its actual local gradient length. Its title and axes disclose the
fixed temperatures/current and absence of a solved edge. Review thresholds
are labeled, not treated as proof of validity. Both final figures require
visual inspection, especially logarithmic labels and layer/profile ranges.
Both assembled figures were visually inspected: measured mesh labels do not
collide, units and legends are readable, the thin-layer artifact is retained,
and the conditional edge assumptions are visible in the figure titles.
