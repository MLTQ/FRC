# Controlled displacement of gas ends

Four nr=64 runs retain the identical two prescribed coils, waveforms, initial
gas, physical eta and compatible RK3 parameters. At a common magnetic matching
radius and half-length of 3 m, compare gas half-lengths 1 and 2 m for both
boundary methods. This separates moving the reflecting gas ends from changing
the magnetic matching surface. The length-1 records also compare with the
original matching extent 2 at the same grid resolution.

The actual gas domain changes; total mass and gas energy therefore change.
Compare response work, currents, and common central spatial windows, not total
gas energy or unlike-domain velocity norms. The end boundaries still reflect
gas: this tests geometric sensitivity, not an open exhaust or free surface.
All mesh/source parameters, accepted budgets, fixed-strip metadata and full
field snapshots use the paired pulse study's recorded conventions. Independent
end-driver, pulse-driver, probe and engine hashes retain provenance. A failure
keeps the accepted diagnostics, and incomplete progress files are separate.
