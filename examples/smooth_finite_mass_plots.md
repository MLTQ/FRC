# Smooth transport accuracy figure

Uses audited initial/final records only. Shows improved slow thermal rates,
unresolved magnetic rates with explicitly labeled oracle controls, full fast-wave
evolution and unmagnetized contact transit including its initial projection error.
Partial contact runs are excluded from same-time convergence and named visibly.
No exact-field substitution is presented as a production correction.

Matplotlib Agg generates a scientific standalone PNG. The report hashes it;
visual inspection is required before documenting it as inspected. Distinct
physical-signal and full-pressure denominators are labeled explicitly.

## Representation labels

The contact panel explicitly distinguishes nodal electron from cell-mean ion
pressure errors. Continuous physical pressure/temperature errors are separate
report audit fields and must not be inferred from this panel.
Logarithmic minor tick labels are suppressed so the declared grid-count labels
remain legible and do not overlap scientific-notation minor labels.
