# Physical-rate verification audit

Requires the frozen engine/reference/driver and all continuum/discrete archives.
Replays continuum residuals, independent axial-resolution/tighter-tolerance
controls, radial transfer quadrature and volume/axis/maximum physical-rate errors.
The three Chebyshev orders are checked separately from the production mesh.

Its scientific figure compares the full physical gdot and the isolated nonlinear
convective contribution after inertia inversion, with the finest axis profile.
It must be inspected together with the earlier intermediate B* forcing figure;
one quantity cannot be substituted for the other. Report/source/figure hashes
are retained. This study verifies instantaneous bounded rates, not a moving
finite-mass integrator, a precision fix, or a physical formation experiment.
