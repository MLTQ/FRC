# Independent joint-transport diagnostics and figure

Rechecks raw hashes, differentiates each physical species pressure/temperature
from actual n/C rates and compares against independent original Euler rates.
Every accepted history contributes mass, all momentum and both entropy-density
drift norms. Species endpoint errors are separate so a joint norm cannot hide
ion/electron failure. Local specific-entropy errors are measured against the
analytic state: global entropy conservation does not imply exact pointwise
preservation of constant specific entropy in a wave. The four-panel figure uses retained continuum rate,
full-transit/wave, and fixed-grid time-control records; no fitted curves or
synthetic data are substituted. Nodal and continuous energy remain distinct.
This helper and its companion are archived separately without changing the
frozen solver/study/audit source identity.
