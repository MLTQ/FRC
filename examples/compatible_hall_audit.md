# Compatible Hall record audit

`collection` requires complete ordered protocol coverage, unchanged engine and
protocol provenance, identical individual/collection JSON, matching NPZ hashes,
finite fields, and actual mesh coordinates. `load_all` joins 54 main cases and
nine independent controls; the latter include their own protocol source hash.

`trajectory` checks every accepted time, true terminal time, step counts,
declared time caps, positive pressure/density, monotonic physical heat, zero
Hall heat, residual tolerance and conducting/periodic/axis flux traces. It
replays initial/final mass, total, magnetic, internal and kinetic energy and
angular momentum. Histories supply maximum rather than only endpoint drift;
the decay protocol has fewer columns, so its mass/angular checks use endpoints.

`magnetic_product` independently integrates the physical cylindrical field
defined by bilinear psi in x=r²/2,z and Btheta=r g using 24 radial Gauss points
and two exact axial product points. It does not call the production energy or
current routines. Spatial rate replay uses B dot Bdot/mu0. Evolved kinetic
energy uses exact annular r² and the documented conservative convention.

`legacy_axis` verifies archived source bytes against commit f28714c before
showing the failed older operator. Historical data are never rehashed as if
generated by the corrected solver. This audit is report-only and cannot edit
or repair archived states. Assertion failure prevents a successful report.
For identically zero spatial magnetic transfer, replay must also be exactly
zero; the audit does not divide zero by zero or invent a relative scale.
