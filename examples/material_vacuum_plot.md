# Saved moving-surface expansion figure

Shows actual initial/final meridional surfaces for spherical and rotating
elongated runs; independent analytic/affine boundaries are overlaid. The lower
panels show surface-position error under refinement and inertia-weighted angular
speed computed from saved coordinates and canonical angular momentum. It does
not prescribe boundary motion or angular speed to the solver.

Only complete successful trajectories are accepted. PNG/PDF and source/run
references are saved; no physical trajectory is rerun. The zero-current Euler
limit and unfinished magnetic/edge-transport scope are explicit on the figure.
This is a single scientific result figure, not a verification framework.

The rotational inertia uses the same SciPy physical electron mass as the run.
