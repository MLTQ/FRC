# Frozen Hall spatial verification protocol

42 explicitly configured instantaneous cases: nonlinear variable-density mesh
refinement through nr=128; constant/stronger-density and zero initial toroidal
or poloidal perturbation controls; order 3/5/7 common quadrature; cell versus
point-sampled carrier density; all three finite-pressure Hall wave branches in
both axial directions on three meshes; and two extra amplitude controls.

nz=2 nr, periodic length .24 m and radius .035 m. The wave species is explicitly
Ar+ with a massless electron and pe=0, positive uniform density and finite ion
pressure. It is a conditional analytic reference, not a selected formation
closure. All cases retain the filled conducting-cylinder boundary assumption.
Nothing in this protocol advances time or certifies nonlinear formation.
