# Independent extracted-response fields

Differentiates the declared physical reconstruction about the stationary saved
contact: conservative density, velocity quotient, both specific entropies,
magnetic field and weak-Ampere current. These are physical tangent fields, not
nonlinear state replacements. `inner` integrates each vector's physical norm.

`localization` integrates radial marginal squared amplitude and independent
continuous axial Fourier coefficients through twice the field grid count.
Reports component, axis/wall-element and outermost-cell fractions, plus the
uncaptured Fourier norm. A large Fourier index alone is not proof of a numerical
mode; piecewise polynomial representations also have unresolved harmonics.
Used by the saved-response direction audit; quadrature control is required.
