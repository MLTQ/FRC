# Paused-project field presentation

Reads only frozen regular-coupling case07 after verifying its raw hash. Samples
all three actual current and magnetic components using the independent compatible
basis. Streamlines follow the complete magnetic vector, including Btheta;
current volume uses |J|. Geometry is scaled uniformly for display; SI legends
and physical cylinder dimensions remain explicit. Streamline interpolation and
optical opacity are presentation approximations, not a finer simulation.

Reuses the archived renderer in a separate standalone output directory, leaving
the earlier FRC poster untouched. This is one verified instantaneous fixture,
not newly evolved formation. Camera orbit is the animation. The localhost server
accepts bounded PNG/WebM exports at two fixed names only. No new physics run.

Exports: 2800×1900 PNG poster, 1600×900 H.264 MP4 camera orbit (about six
seconds), WebM recording, self-contained HTML and raw display data. The browser
page, poster and extracted movie frame were visually inspected; MP4 dimensions
and duration were checked. `presentation_check.json` records export hashes
and verifies the complete frozen coupling calculation source and raw fields.
The page title identifies the verified checkpoint.
