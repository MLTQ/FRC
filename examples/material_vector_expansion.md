# Full-vector heated material expansion

Runs a complete simultaneous trajectory of all axisymmetric magnetic/current/
fluid components, including angle/angular momentum, both species entropies and
actual heating/exchange. It uses the same compact density/temperature and free
side/end geometry as the preceding material runs. All accepted states and source
closure are preserved, with explicit resource/physical equation rejection.

Initial poloidal flux is psi=(Bp/2)*r²*hr^4*hz^4; Btheta=Bt*r/R*hr³*hz³.
Here hr=1-r²/R², hz=1-z²/L². Their analytic B and curl(B)/mu0 are supplied together
to the physical magnetic/carrier initial projection. Both field and current
vanish at the boundary. The exterior contains exactly no field or matter.
This initialized flux is a component trajectory, not coil-driven formation.

The independent initial force is -grad(p)+J cross B-div(mu*n*w*w), including
all cylindrical stress connections and toroidal acceleration. Complex steps
differentiate only these explicit continuum reference formulas. The production
force/torque comes instead from magnetic/current virtual work on the evolving
mesh. Initial field/current and particle-weighted acceleration errors are retained.

The independent full-vector virial is I''=4E-2Wb for gamma=5/3, including toroidal
bulk kinetic and all current energy. Its separate magnetic time integral is not
fed back into evolution. Axial momentum and total bulk angular momentum should
remain constant with no external source; actual residuals are recorded. Local
rotation, field components and current components remain actual evolving states.

First-order upwind entropy diffusion is explicit. Constant eta and conditional
Maxwellian temperature exchange remain declared approximations; no edge heat,
collision check or residual tolerance is removed. Minimum entropy and actual
transport outflow rate accompany the complete trajectory. Spatial accuracy,
physical edge/ionization, exterior coil coupling and formation remain open.

The first complete4x8,p3,q6,2000-step run has initial B/J errors19.6%/35.9%,
energy drift2.33e-6 and virial error2.01e-7. Rotation is generated by its actual
torque. A6x12,p5,q10 trajectory is pending; initial B/J errors are0.432%/0.844%.
