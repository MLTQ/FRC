# Independent continuous coupling audit

Reconstructs compatible fields using the existing independent root-product
derivatives, regular scalars using normalized polynomial roots, and axial
samples through complex FFT coefficients with the real Nyquist cosine. None
of the production map's sample, adjoint, force or current-energy methods is
called. Positive density and actual physical velocity are checked at independent
quadrature. It integrates Lorentz work and local current/magnetic energy rates
from saved coefficients and the actual supplied continuity rate.

Higher physical quadrature separates integration error from force/induction
truncation error. The density exchange remains a measured missing term in the
incomplete moving equations; matching that exchange is not full conservation.
