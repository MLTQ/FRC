# Conservative bulk thermal rate protocol

Fourteen cases span degree 3/5, four resolutions, physical quadrature, density,
opposite flow, exact constant entropy and stronger density contrast. Actual
particle-momentum means supply a single face flux and continuity derivative.
Both species use independent analytic pressure/density gradients as rate oracles.

Preserves all state/rate/face/work arrays, original scalar residuals, physical
entropy and weak-continuity defects, independent local energy and quadrature
checks. No rate is fitted to the reference. Exceptions are saved and fail the
protocol; fresh output and unchanged source closure are mandatory.
