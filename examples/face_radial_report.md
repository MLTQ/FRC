# Common radial-face scientific figure

Reads saved old/new original-equation audits, separated near-axis force norms,
physical energy-rate defects, independent Bessel frequency checks and the actual
full-transit contact history. It does not rerun or synthesize data. Six panels
distinguish force accuracy, linear-wave behavior and energy accounting.

Individual force comparisons use the first annulus: each individual pressure
or centrifugal force need not satisfy the imposed impermeable outer-wall
constraint, whereas their total does for this reference. Their unrestricted
whole-domain comparison errors remain in the raw rate records. The figure
does not claim a nonlinear energy theorem or a validated formation experiment.
Logarithmic mesh axes show only the actual tested cell counts, with minor
tick labels suppressed so the resolution labels cannot overlap.
