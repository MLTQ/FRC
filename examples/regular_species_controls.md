# Discontinuous traces and complete frozen spectra

Retains the handoff point-transfer unsplit proposal, its naive skew variant,
and the physical-projection skew candidate. Builds each full small frozen
operator in the actual sqrt(V*n)-weighted entropy coordinates. Records real
growth, symmetric part, constant-state defect and an exact matrix-exponential
quadratic test for a reproducibly randomized discontinuous state. No spectrum
is filtered and no real part is discarded.

Both species' discontinuous entropy, quadratic and thermal/pressure budgets are
measured. A finite small frozen spectrum is not a nonlinear stability proof;
the algebraic skew/constant contract is stated separately. Random discontinuous
traces test conservation and bounded frozen evolution, not a shock solution or
an entropy-production inequality.

The saved `constant_rate_scaled` divides the weighted constant-state rate only
by the operator norm, so it carries sqrt(particle) units. The report separately
computes the dimensionless constant-state backward error from the saved matrix
and the actual sqrt(V*n) vector; the raw metric is not silently relabeled.
