# Warm adiabatic protocol

Thirty declared successful evolution cases: contact and nonlinear simple wave
at nz16/32/64/128/256; each has half-CFL, gamma1.4, first-order and four
fixed-grid time controls (128/256/512/2048 steps through .05 periods).
Contact adds reverse velocity, stronger density/temperature, reference-density
scale, radial-resolution and nearly cold-electron limits. The simple wave
adds Mach .12 compression before characteristic crossing. Base contact evolves
one full transit; base simple wave evolves half an acoustic transit. No case
is allowed to disappear or become an implicit expected failure.

Every reference and numerical parameter is expanded into the saved case.
These are ideal filled-cylinder tests, not experimental validity/formation.
