# Independent physical-volume reconstruction errors

Positive tensor Gauss integration samples the actual solver's limited density,
regular vtheta=r Omega and Btheta=r g reconstruction at physical r,z points.
The independent point reference supplies true continuum fields. This includes
subcell reconstruction error and is distinct from comparing cell averages.
The integral weights are 2 pi r dr dz, including the axis annulus. None of the
numeric fields is fitted to the analytic reference. Orders 6 and 10 separately
estimate quadrature uncertainty; zero reference fields have no relative norm.
Pressure blending does not affect these three sampled components.
