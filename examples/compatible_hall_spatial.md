# Compatible Hall spatial measurements

Uses the independent analytic variable-density snapshot and true conservative
averages. Measures physical current/induction, conservative g, first-annulus g,
corner psi and local compatible-energy versus Poynting transfer errors. Global
Hall work is normalized independently. Density is sampled from the exact
analytic fixture, and reference quadrature orders 10/14 are compared.

Archives actual/reference rates, full initial psi/g, electric coefficients and
both local energy rates. These are instantaneous cases; the separate evolution
module records propagated compressible states and accepted step histories.
