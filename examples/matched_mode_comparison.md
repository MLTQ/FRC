meshes and duration. Verifies audited summary/raw hashes, reconstructs both
centered physical responses independently at quadrature16/20, and reports
whole/axis/wall/max differences in all nine fields, both pressures, current and
full E. Norm denominators are the first actual physical response, not absolute
background fields. Differences expose time/amplitude dependence without fitting
a growth rate or production eigenmode. One pair of times does not establish an
order of convergence.

Stores complete input/source identities and fingerprints of reconstructible
physical samples. A separate fresh invocation can replay every non-time metric.
This comparison changes no state and does not certify unresolved interface,
vacuum, formation or laboratory confinement physics.
