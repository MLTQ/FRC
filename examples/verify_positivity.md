# Low-pressure verification artifact

`verify` measures coarse low-background-pressure equilibrium stress cases and
independent magnetized double rarefactions across grids. It retains parameters,
versions, source fingerprint, limiter activity, field data and conservation.
The central pressure error is recorded separately from bulk L1 norms so a
positive, visually plausible profile cannot hide poor relative core accuracy.

`plot` shows density, logarithmic pressure, refinement and an initial limiter
map. `report` distinguishes robustness from resolution and a reconstruction
guarantee from a full-update theorem. Artifacts are local numerical evidence,
not a physical operating regime or a demonstrated formation experiment.
