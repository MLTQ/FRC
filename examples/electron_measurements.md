# Electron pressure measurements

Runs the declared protocol sequentially under one frozen source fingerprint.
Spatial metrics compare against independent physical current line/annular
integrals, analytic pressure battery and electron entropy derivatives, including
axis and wall. Order10/14 continuum averaging checks the reference uncertainty.
Zero physical battery has an absolute RMS error; its relative norm is null.

Evolutions archive initial/final g, positive dual density/pressure/entropy proxy,
physical volumes and all accepted invariant histories. Drift has an independent
exact translating electron pressure and unchanged magnetic field. The pressure
battery starts with g=0 and evolves coupled electron thermal feedback; nonlinear
runs start with finite g. Time counts, nonlinear residuals and iteration totals
are explicit. The expected precision-failure control preserves accepted input
arrays and records its actual partial time. All other failures prevent collection
completion. Success does not certify bulk-fluid, vacuum or formation physics.
