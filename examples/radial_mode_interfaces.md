# Current representation and exact internal traces

Measures the fixed analytic mode's initial weak current, independently
reconstructed strong curl(B)/mu0, and strong current divergence. Strong-Ampere
relative error uses the strong-current norm; it excludes distributional surface
terms and is not the complete weak identity. The analytic current provides a
separate unambiguous consistency target for each representation.

Exact selected left/right polynomial traces measure normal current and
tangential B against the same analytic mode at every internal radial/axial
face. Includes the periodic seam; excludes the exterior wall and zero-area
axis. Reports each side, jumps normalized by the analytic face norm, absolute
jump maxima, per-face squared integrals and physical fingerprints. It never
averages traces or moves the evaluation point. These are spatial consistency
diagnostics, not local energy or charge-state evolution repairs.
