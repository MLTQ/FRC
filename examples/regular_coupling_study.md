# Measured regular-fluid/current coupling

Each case verifies physical force accuracy at all active points and the first
element, grid kinetic work against independent physical force work, total
axial force and angular torque. The original analytic magnetic/current fixture
then tests Ampere and finite-inertia induction with actual fluid continuity.
Current energy density gradients are checked by complex differentiation of the
physical energy integral. Source/raw arrays and residuals are retained.

The ideal/Hall pair is also solved with that actual continuity rate. Its full
energy remainder is explicitly compared with the still unresolved density
exchange; an incomplete system is not labeled energy conserving. There is no
time evolution, diffusion, physical heating or atmosphere. Degree, separate
fluid/field resolutions and quadrature controls are explicit in every record.
