# Rejection evidence figure

Plots every accepted minimum ion pressure and separately marks the rejected
trial at each of the three time resolutions. The nr64 control shows the maximum
local error retained under time refinement. No line extends rejected states
to the requested final time, and no pressure is clipped for presentation.
The continuum symbol is discussed in the text, not presented as a fit to these
axis-local failures. Output must be visually inspected and hashed by its report.
