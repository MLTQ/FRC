# Independent references for graded material meshes

Reuses the independently derived spherical magnetic-decay/current/heat solution
and exact homologous free-gas expansion, with explicit nonuniform radial shells.
Seven decay cases separate grading, angular and uniform-refinement effects;
six gas cases measure position, velocity, density, mass and response error.
Geometry, physics, time caps, versions/source fingerprint and lossless initial/
final or initial/mid/final arrays are recorded. No source file may change while
these measurements run. Partial cases cannot become completed study evidence.

Gas expansion has p=1-s², rho=1 and lambda=sqrt(1+2t²); it has no heating and
does not validate a heated surface with initially nonzero boundary current.
Diffusion holds material positions fixed. Their purposes are independent
component accuracy and regressions under grading, not formation validation.
