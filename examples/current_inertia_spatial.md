# Nonlinear current-inertia spatial measurement

Preserves the actual original toroidal B and varying n. Compares independent
analytic curl-w and nonlinear convective electric fields with the production
weak current, projection and B* construction. Separately compares the resulting
g forcing against continuum annular averages of the full physical electric curl.
The field is non-Beltrami, so nonlinear inertia need not cancel as in the wave.

Five resolutions include nr128 and explicitly retain first-annulus and maximum
errors. This forcing is for B*, before inversion of the current-inertia operator;
it must not be mislabeled instantaneous Bdot. Full reference, physical masses,
quadratures, lossless forcing fields, engine and helper source hashes are stored.
This supplemental measurement never modifies or rehashes the 41-case protocol.
